#TLDR

Airbnb length of stay discounts cut your nightly rate when guests book longer trips. Weekly discounts apply at 7+ nights. Monthly discounts apply at 28+ nights. You can also add custom trip-length tiers (for example 3 or 5 nights). Discounts of 10% or more show in search results. Many hosts start near 5-15% weekly and 15-30% monthly, then raise or lower the offer by season. Longer stays usually mean fewer turnovers, lower cleaning load, and steadier occupancy when you price the discount against real turnover cost.

## What Airbnb Length of Stay Discounts Are

Airbnb length of stay discounts reward guests who book more nights at a lower effective nightly rate. Airbnb documents weekly discounts for stays of 7 or more nights and monthly discounts for stays of 28 or more nights. You set the percentage in Price settings under Discounts. Guests see the savings next to your original price in the price breakdown. Discounts of 10% or more also appear in search results. After any discount, your minimum daily price must stay at least $10 per day.

Source: [Airbnb Help: Weekly and monthly discounts for home stays](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1233)

You can go beyond the basic weekly and monthly toggles. Professional hosting tools and rule-sets support length-of-stay discounts across more trip lengths, including ranges such as 2 to 6 nights, multi-week weekly tiers, and multi-week monthly tiers. That lets you reward a 3-night or 5-night booking without waiting for a full week.

Sources: [Airbnb Help: How rule-sets work](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2061), [Airbnb Community: How to put out a discount for 3 days](https://community.withairbnb.com/t5/Support-with-your-bookings/How-to-put-out-a-discount-for-3-days/td-p/682025)

## Why Longer Stays Often Raise Net Earnings

A longer booking is not only a lower sticker rate. It changes your cost stack:

- Fewer check-ins and check-outs mean fewer full cleans.
- Fewer restocks and fewer calendar gaps between short stays.
- One longer reservation reduces the risk of several short stays canceling or leaving odd empty nights.

Professional operators treat length of stay discounts as a way to buy occupancy and cut turnover work, not as a permanent base-rate cut. Guides for multi-channel hosts commonly frame weekly offers around 5-15% and monthly offers around 15-30%, then adjust for market strength and operating cost.

Source: [Uplisting: Smart Airbnb Discount Strategies for Hosts](https://www.uplisting.io/blog/airbnb-discount-strategies-uplisting)

Run the math before you copy a neighbor’s percentage. If a full turnover costs you $150 in cleaner time, supplies, and your own hours, two 3-night stays create two turnovers. One 6-night stay creates one. A modest length-of-stay cut can still leave you ahead once you subtract real turnover cost from each short stay.

## How to Set Weekly, Monthly, and Custom Trip Length Discounts

### Basic weekly and monthly discounts (desktop)

Airbnb’s help flow for desktop:

1. Open **Calendar** and select the listing.
2. Open **Price settings**, then **Discounts**.
3. Choose **Weekly**, **Monthly**, or **Trip length** discounts.
4. Enter the percentage and save.

Source: [Airbnb Help: Weekly and monthly discounts for home stays](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1233)

### Custom length tiers (3 nights, 5 nights, and similar)

Hosts who want a 3-night or similar custom tier often use **Length of stay discounts** in Pricing, then **Add a custom discount** and pick the night count. Community threads describe this path for 3-night offers. Rule-sets under professional hosting tools expand the same idea across more night ranges and multiple listings.

Sources: [Airbnb Community: How to put out a discount for 3 days](https://community.withairbnb.com/t5/Support-with-your-bookings/How-to-put-out-a-discount-for-3-days/td-p/682025), [Airbnb Help: How rule-sets work](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2061)

### Rule-sets and Smart Pricing

If you use rule-sets, Airbnb applies pricing rules in a defined order. Nightly and weekend pricing apply first. Length-of-stay discounts apply next. Early-bird and last-minute discounts come after that inside the rule-set calculation examples Airbnb publishes. Smart Pricing overrides rule-sets, so turn Smart Pricing off when you want rule-sets to control those dates.

Source: [Airbnb Help: How rule-sets work](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2061)

## Which Discount Percentages Hosts Actually Use

Airbnb does not force a single “correct” weekly or monthly percentage. Hosts set the number. Patterns that show up often in host discussions and operator guides:

| Stay length | Common starting range | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly (7+ nights) | About 5-15% | High-demand urban listings often need less. |
| Two-week style tiers | About 10-15% | Some hosts add a mid tier between weekly and monthly. |
| Monthly (28+ nights) | About 15-30% | Deeper only when shoulder season or low occupancy justifies it. |
| Custom 3-5 nights | Small single-digit to low teens | Useful when you want slightly longer mini-stays without a full week. |

One professional-host discussion example cited a structure near 10% weekly, 15% for two weeks, and 25% monthly while holding above-market rates and mid-60s to 70% occupancy. Treat that as a case study, not a rule.

Source: [Facebook Professional Hosts discussion on typical monthly discounts](https://www.facebook.com/groups/professionalhosts/posts/9337604993002776/)

Start conservative. Raise the discount only when calendar gaps stay open. Cut the discount (or remove it on peak dates via rule-sets) when you already fill weeks at full rate.

## How Airbnb Prioritizes Multiple Discounts

When several discounts could apply, Airbnb does not always stack every percentage. Airbnb’s Resource Center lists this priority order for which discount wins on a night:

1. New listing promotion  
2. Custom promotion  
3. Length-of-stay discount  
4. Early-bird discount  
5. Last-minute discount  

Only the highest-priority discount in that list applies per night in the examples Airbnb gives. A custom promotion can beat a deeper monthly length-of-stay discount. Rule-sets still set the base nightly price first; then the winning discount applies to that price. A non-refundable option can apply an extra cut after other discounts in Airbnb’s published examples.

Source: [Airbnb Resource Center: Combining discounts and rule-sets](https://www.airbnb.com/resources/hosting-homes/a/combining-discounts-and-rule-sets-694)

Practical takeaway: audit live bookings. Confirm the final nightly rate matches the priority order you expect. If you run a steep custom promo and a monthly length-of-stay discount at the same time, guests may receive the promo path, not the monthly path.

Host community threads sometimes report stacking behavior that differs from the Resource Center examples. When payout math looks wrong, open the reservation price breakdown and contact Airbnb Support with screenshots rather than guessing.

## Search Visibility and Airbnb Monthly Stay Savings

Two visibility details matter for hosts who use Airbnb length of stay discounts:

- **Search badges:** Discounts of 10% or more display in search results. Smaller discounts still show in the listing price breakdown but may not earn the same search callout.
- **Airbnb-funded monthly stay savings:** In some cases Airbnb adds monthly stay savings on 28+ night trips. Airbnb states those savings are paid by Airbnb and are not deducted from your payout. Guests can receive that incentive on top of your own discount or promotion.

Source: [Airbnb Help: Weekly and monthly discounts for home stays](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1233)

If you want the search callout, set at least one length-of-stay tier at 10% or higher on the dates you care about. If you prefer quiet discounts that only appear in the breakdown, stay under 10%.

## Seasonal Strategy: When to Deepen or Pause Discounts

Length-of-stay offers work hardest when empty nights cost you more than a moderate rate cut.

**Lean harder on discounts when:**

- Shoulder or off-season gaps sit open week after week.
- Cleaner capacity or your own time makes frequent turnovers painful.
- You want mid-length remote-work or relocation stays to anchor the calendar.

**Pull back or block deep discounts when:**

- Peak weekends and local events already book at full rate.
- A major demand spike is coming and longer stays at a deep cut would block higher-yield short stays.
- Your occupancy already sits high and discounts only train guests to wait for deals.

High-demand event windows deserve special care. Hosts near large sports and mega-event calendars often keep full-rate short stays for peak nights and reserve stronger weekly or monthly offers for the quieter weeks around those peaks. Long Beach and LA hosts planning toward large multi-week demand periods can study real local inventory strategy at [longbeacholympics.com](https://longbeacholympics.com) as one example of event-era positioning while they still use length-of-stay tools on ordinary months.

Off-season host forums often describe monthly discounts near 25% only when winter or slow months need fill, then lighter or zero monthly cuts in peak season.

Source: [Airhostsforum: Suggested Discounts](https://airhostsforum.com/t/suggested-discounts/49316)

## Step-by-Step Setup Checklist

1. **Price your base night first.** Length-of-stay math fails if your rack rate sits far below the market.
2. **List true turnover cost.** Cleaner fee, laundry, supplies, and your hours per turn.
3. **Pick tiers.** Example starter set: 5-10% at 7 nights, optional mid tier near 14 nights, 15-25% at 28 nights.
4. **Decide on custom short tiers.** Add 3- or 5-night custom discounts only if you want to nudge mini-stays longer without a full week.
5. **Set discounts in Calendar → Price settings → Discounts** (and rule-sets if you use professional tools).
6. **Review discount priority** against custom promos, early-bird, and last-minute tools so you do not undercut yourself.
7. **Test a sample booking** for 7 nights and 28 nights. Confirm the guest total and your payout.
8. **Revisit monthly.** Raise, cut, or seasonally pause tiers based on occupancy and average daily rate.

## Common Mistakes That Quietly Cut Revenue

**Deep monthly cuts on peak calendars.** A 30-50% monthly discount during high season can erase the revenue you would earn from several strong short stays.

**Ignoring cleaner math.** If short stays already pay for themselves after cleaning fees, you may not need aggressive weekly cuts.

**Stacking tools without a plan.** Custom promotions sit above length-of-stay discounts in Airbnb’s published priority list. A flash promo can replace the weekly structure you thought guests would receive.

**Never testing custom 3-5 night tiers.** Some markets book many 2-night trips. A small 3-night incentive can reduce one-night gaps without giving away a full weekly cut.

**Leaving the same percentages all year.** Static discounts ignore seasonality. Update rule-sets or discount settings when demand shifts.

## FAQ

### Do weekly and monthly discounts both apply on a 28-night stay?

Airbnb’s published priority model applies one winning discount type per night among competing discount categories, with length-of-stay sitting above early-bird and last-minute in that list. Confirm the reservation breakdown on real bookings. If two length tiers somehow interact in a way you did not expect, document it and contact Support.

Sources: [Airbnb Resource Center: Combining discounts and rule-sets](https://www.airbnb.com/resources/hosting-homes/a/combining-discounts-and-rule-sets-694), [Airbnb Community thread on weekly and monthly discounts](https://community.withairbnb.com/t5/Help-with-your-business/Airbnb-applied-both-weekly-and-monthly-discounts-in-the-same/m-p/1511597)

### Will a length of stay discount show in Airbnb search?

Discounts of 10% or more display in search results. All discounts appear next to the original price in the listing price breakdown.

Source: [Airbnb Help: Weekly and monthly discounts for home stays](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1233)

### Can I offer a discount for 3 nights only?

Yes. Use custom length-of-stay discount options in Pricing, or configure trip-length rules inside professional rule-sets where available.

Sources: [Airbnb Community: How to put out a discount for 3 days](https://community.withairbnb.com/t5/Support-with-your-bookings/How-to-put-out-a-discount-for-3-days/td-p/682025), [Airbnb Help: How rule-sets work](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2061)

### Do early-bird or last-minute discounts stack on top of weekly discounts?

Airbnb’s Resource Center states that when multiple discounts compete, only the highest-priority discount applies per night, and length-of-stay ranks above early-bird and last-minute. Some host community posts describe different live behavior. Trust the reservation price breakdown over assumptions.

Sources: [Airbnb Resource Center: Combining discounts and rule-sets](https://www.airbnb.com/resources/hosting-homes/a/combining-discounts-and-rule-sets-694), [Airbnb Community: do discounts stack?](https://community.withairbnb.com/t5/Support-with-your-bookings/do-discounts-stack/m-p/2145971/highlight/true)

### Does Airbnb automatically give guests 30% off monthly stays?

No required automatic host-funded 30% monthly discount applies to every listing. You choose your monthly percentage. Separately, Airbnb may fund monthly stay savings on some 28+ night trips without taking that portion from your payout.

Sources: [Airbnb Help: Weekly and monthly discounts for home stays](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1233), [Airhostsforum: Standard Monthly Discount](https://airhostsforum.com/t/standard-monthly-discount/44621)

## Conclusion

Airbnb length of stay discounts help you trade a controlled nightly cut for fewer turnovers and fuller calendars. Set weekly and monthly percentages that still clear your costs after cleaning. Add custom 3- or 5-night tiers if your market lives on short trips. Watch Airbnb’s discount priority order so promotions do not silently replace the structure you designed. Review the settings each season, especially around high-demand event windows.
