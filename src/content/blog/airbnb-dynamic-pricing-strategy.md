# TLDR

Dynamic pricing adjusts your nightly rate automatically based on demand, local events, seasonality, and competitor availability. Setting a static nightly price is one of the biggest revenue leaks in short-term rental hosting, most hosts leave 20% to 40% on the table by underpricing peak dates and overpricing slow weekdays. This guide covers how to set your baseline rate, build a dynamic pricing strategy, leverage automated pricing tools like PriceLabs, and apply strategic discounts to maximize occupancy and revenue.

---

## Understanding Your Local STR Market

Before you set a single price or configure automated rules, you need a clear baseline of what your local market will bear. Most hosts either guess or blindly copy a nearby competitor without knowing whether that competitor is actually booked.

**How to research your market effectively:**

1. **Filter by apples-to-apples comps:** Search Airbnb for listings with the exact same bedroom and bathroom count in your immediate neighborhood.
2. **Examine calendar availability:** A competitor listed at $150/night with a fully booked calendar indicates high demand at that price. A competitor at $100/night with an empty calendar is overpriced relative to their listing's quality or reviews.
3. **Focus on high-review listings:** Established listings with 20+ reviews reflect your real competition. Ignore unreviewed or brand-new listings when benchmarking standard rates.
4. **Inspect a 90-day horizon:** Don't just look at this weekend. Track how prices fluctuate across peak months, holidays, and low-season weekdays.

Repeat this comp research quarterly to adapt to changing local supply and seasonal demand shifts.

## Setting Your Baseline Nightly Rate

Your baseline price is your default weekday rate during average demand periods. Every dynamic adjustment, weekend surges, last-minute discounts, and event spikes, calculates off this anchor.

* **For New Listings:** Set your baseline **10% to 15% below** comparable reviewed listings for your first 5-10 bookings. Sacrificing early margin earns you initial 5-star reviews, which unlock full pricing power long-term.
* **For Established Listings:** Aim for a target occupancy of **70% to 85%**. If your occupancy consistently exceeds 90%, you are underpriced. If it drops below 60%, your baseline is too high or your listing needs visual/copy optimization.
* **Calculate Your Absolute Price Floor:** Determine your exact break-even cost per night, including cleaning fees, utility estimates, platform commissions, guest supplies, and mortgage/rent. **Never allow automated pricing tools to drop below this minimum floor.**

## The Core Elements of Dynamic Pricing

Static pricing (charging the same flat rate every night) guarantees you lose money: you miss out on high guest willingness-to-pay during surge dates and stay empty on low-demand Tuesdays.

A complete dynamic pricing model adjusts according to three main variables:

### 1. Day of the Week

* **Weekends (Friday & Saturday):** Command 30% to 50% above your weekday baseline in most leisure markets.
* **Mid-Week (Sunday–Thursday):** Require lower rates to attract longer stays or remote workers, unless you operate in a corporate-driven business travel market.

### 2. Booking Lead Time

* **60+ Days Out:** Keep rates at market rate or slightly above to capture early, non-price-sensitive bookers.
* **14-30 Days Out:** Hold standard market rates as your primary booking window opens.
* **Under 7 Days Out:** Automatically apply modest last-minute discounts (10%–15%) to fill remaining unbooked nights, an empty night generates $0.

### 3. Seasonality and Event Spikes

Local events (concerts, festivals, sporting events, conferences) are where hosts see the highest revenue spikes.
* Build a local event calendar 90 days ahead.
* For major regional draws, increase rates by **2x to 3x** your normal baseline at least 60-90 days in advance.

## Automated Dynamic Pricing Tools Compared

Managing daily price adjustments manually is time-consuming and inefficient. Third-party dynamic pricing software syncs directly with Airbnb and VRBO to update rates daily:

* **PriceLabs:** The industry standard for independent hosts and property managers. Offers powerful customization rules, hyper-local market data, minimum-stay controls, and price floors. Costs ~$19.99/month per listing.
* **Wheelhouse:** Features an intuitive interface that balances automated algorithms with custom aggressiveness settings (Conservative, Recommended, or Aggressive).
* **Beyond (formerly Beyond Pricing):** Designed for larger portfolios and property management systems with percentage-based revenue pricing.
* **Airbnb Smart Pricing (Built-In):** Free and convenient, but inherently conservative. Because Airbnb's primary goal is maximizing total bookings (and platform transaction count) rather than host profit, Smart Pricing frequently underprices listings. If you use it, always set a strict minimum price floor.

## Strategic Discounts That Increase Total Revenue

Discounts should protect your profit margin while driving booking velocity:

* **Length-of-Stay Discounts:** Offer a 10%–15% weekly discount and a 20%–30% monthly discount. Fewer turnovers reduce cleaning logistics, linen wear, and utility spikes.
* **Last-Minute Discounter:** Trigger a 10% discount inside 3 days of arrival to capture spontaneous travelers.
* **Early-Bird Incentive:** Offer a 5% discount for bookings made 90+ days in advance to lock in foundational calendar occupancy.
